# Training Mode

Training Mode is a practice field with **full match rules**. Pick a situation (a play-the-ball on the fourth tackle
just inside halfway, a scrum, a penalty goal, a goal line drop-out), play it out, and the game resets it the moment the
play ends. Run it again as many times as you like.

It's built for learning: practise your attack against different defences, work on your goal kicking, test a set
play, or see how a player's ratings change what he can do.

## Opening Training Mode

From the main menu: **Play > Training Mode**.

The setup page has three tabs. Switch with **LB / RB** (L1 / R1): **Basic**, **Teams** and **Customise**. Every tab
starts with **Start Training** and ends with **Reset All to Defaults**. Your choices are remembered for next time.

## Setting up a rep

The **Basic** tab sets up the situation every rep starts from.

| Setting | Choices |
|---|---|
| **Rep Type** | Play-the-Ball, Scrum, Penalty Tap, 20m Restart, Kick for Touch, Penalty Goal, Conversion, Kick-Off, Goal Line Drop-Out, 20m Drop-Out |
| **You Play** | Attack or Defend |
| **Attackers / Defenders** | From a full 13 down to 3 attackers and 1 defender (the marker). Players you don't need sit out the rep. |
| **Attacking From** | Your own 10 m line through halfway to the opposition 10 m line |
| **Ruck Position** | Left Edge, Left, Middle, Right, Right Edge (from the attacking team's view) |
| **Tackle** | Fresh Set, After Tackle 1 to 4, or Last Tackle |
| **Defence Formation** | Standard, Compress, Slide Left, Slide Right, Drop Wingers |
| **Attack Tactic** | Your Call, Hit Up, Cross Over, Out The Back, Inside Ball |
| **Difficulty** | Rookie to Legend |
| **Player Stats** | On / Off: the live stats card (see below) |
| **Stats Shown** | All ratings or just the Key ones |

You always play as the home team. On kick-offs and drop-outs, "Attack" means your team takes the kick.

> Fewer players makes a great drill. Try **3 attackers against 1 defender** to practise a two-on-one, or a full
> attack against **only the marker** to learn a set play's running lines.

## Teams and match settings

- **Teams:** pick each side's league, team and jersey. The default is Broncos v Storm.
- **Customise:** the same options as Play Now: stadium, time of day, weather, ball, field markings, scoreboard,
  referee jersey, referee and touch judges, referee voices and injuries.

## Controls in a rep

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Select / View** | Reset the rep instantly, without pausing |
| **Start / Menu** | Pause menu |

Everything else uses your normal match controls.

## When a rep resets

The rep **resets itself the moment the play ends**:

- a try (before the video referee or celebration);
- a knock-on, a penalty or a six again;
- the ball going into touch or dead;
- a drop-out or a tap restart;
- the last-tackle turnover;
- a field goal, or a captain's challenge;
- the defence winning the ball in open play.

Normal tackles keep the set going: you play the full set from your starting tackle, so a rep that starts on "After
Tackle 3" gives you tackles 4, 5 and 6. Injuries and half time play on. The score and clock carry on in the
background (halves are 40 minutes), and there are **no automatic replays** to sit through.

## The pause menu

**Resume, Reset Rep, Rep Setup, Replay, Controls, Settings, Exit.**

- **Reset Rep:** start the rep again from the beginning.
- **Replay:** watch the last play again, as in a normal match.
- **Rep Setup:** change the drill without leaving the field. It has three tabs (LB / RB):
  - **Rep:** the same rows as the Basic tab, plus **Difficulty**;
  - **Player Edit:** change any player's ratings (see below);
  - **Settings:** turn the stats card on or off, or switch between All and Key stats.

Press **Back** in Rep Setup to apply. If you changed the rep or a player, it saves, resets the rep and drops you
straight back into play. Difficulty and stats card changes apply without a reset.

## Live player stats

With **Player Stats** on, a card on the right of the screen shows the player you control:

- his **live stamina and sprint**, updating as you play;
- his **ratings**: either all 25 (physical, evasion, defence, handling and kicking) or the 11 key ones.

Switch players and the card follows. It's a quick way to see why one forward makes metres and another doesn't.

## Player Edit: change a player's ratings

**Pause > Rep Setup > Player Edit**: pick the **Team**, the **Player**, the **Rating** and set its **Value** (0 to
100). Changes apply from the next rep. **Reset This Player** and **Reset All Edits** put the ratings back.

Edits only last for this training session: they're cleared when you start Training Mode again and never touch your
saved players or squads.

> Want to know what speed really does? Give your winger 100 sprint, then 50, and run the same rep twice.

## Ideas for drills

| Drill | Setup |
|---|---|
| **Last-tackle kicking** | Play-the-Ball, Attack, Last Tackle, Opposition 10m. Practise bombs, grubbers and chips into the in-goal. |
| **Defend a set** | Play-the-Ball, Defend, Fresh Set, Opposition 10m (the attack starts 40 m out). Hold them out for six. |
| **Edge raids** | Play-the-Ball, Attack, Ruck Position Left Edge or Right Edge, Defence Formation Slide Left or Right. |
| **Two-on-one** | Play-the-Ball, Attack, 3 attackers, 1 defender. |
| **Goal kicking** | Penalty Goal or Conversion, then try different stadiums, weather and wind. |
| **Kick-off chase** | Kick-Off, Defend, to practise taking the ball and the first hit-up. |
| **Test a play** | Choose an Attack Tactic, put only the marker on defence, and watch the running lines. |
