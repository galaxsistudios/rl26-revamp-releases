> **Where are all the settings?** Most gameplay changes can be tuned in **Academy > Custom Difficulties**. Edit a
> custom difficulty and you'll find the stock tabs plus the Revamp's **MODDED**, **KICKING** and **LINE BREAKS**
> tabs. L1 / R1 switch tabs, L2 / R2 move a slider ten steps, and Square imports or exports the difficulty as a file.
> The preset difficulties (Rookie to Legend) always use the Revamp's default values.

## The big ones

If you only read one section, read this one.

- **[Discipline meter](discipline-system.md).** Every player has a mental bar beside his stamina. Pressure, fatigue
  and mistakes drain it, and low discipline means errors, penalties, sin bins and send-offs.
- **[Training Mode](training-mode.md).** Play > Training Mode: practise any situation under full match rules, with
  instant resets.
- **Run your own scrums.** Pick the scrum position and feed it yourself, against a shot clock.
- **Real knock-on advantage.** The other team has to make ground before advantage is over, as in the NRL.
- **Aim your kicks.** A predicted flight line for grubbers, field goals, bombs and punts, steered with the left stick.
- **Smarter CPU.** It kicks on the last, uses its whole bench, rotates the fullback as a sweeper and takes two-point
  field goals at the right moments.
- **A TV broadcast look.** NRL-style scoreboard, a "versus" match intro, a coin toss, bunker decisions with TRY / NO
  TRY graphics, team songs and an NRL siren.
- **A bigger career.** Theme rounds, Magic Round, Las Vegas, rivals, fines and suspensions, salary cap sanctions,
  mid-season transfers and a Pacific international calendar.
- **Up-to-date squads and kits.** 2027 player movements, the Perth Bears, NSW Cup squads, hundreds of extra kits, HD
  club logos and corrected club colours.

## On the field: attack

- **Your attack calls actually run.** The play you pick (Hit Up, Cross Over, Out The Back, Inside Ball, Kick) now runs
  across nearly the whole field. Support runners hold better lanes, the backs stand less deep and the halves and
  fullback stop hogging the ball.
- **Better passing.**
  - L1 / R1 no longer flip direction when the broadcast camera pans into a corner.
  - No more passes to a player behind you, into empty space, or to someone heading to the bench.
  - Passes are a little faster and bend with your left stick.
  - Bad passes depend on the passer's rating, and forward passes are called more strictly.
  - Receivers run onto the ball, and support runners hold their lines while you wait.
- **New dummy controls.** While holding a pass button, click **L3** to dummy left or **R3** to dummy right. The old
  dummy still works.
- **Quick play-the-ball.** **Press the right trigger** when you're tackled to play the ball quickly (one press is
  enough). It works at any point of the tackle, including the tackle that ends a line break, and the CPU always plays
  it quickly after a line break. Quick and slow play-the-balls now really change speed (a stock bug meant they didn't).
- **Line breaks.** CPU runners who break clean usually score where they broke instead of always curving to the posts,
  and they get support runners and a pass when a defender closes in.
- **Try-line spills.** A player diving over in contact can lose the ball as he grounds it, and the video referee will
  rule it a knock-on. Disciplined players hold on more often.
- **Position-based passing ratings.** Halves and hookers pass best, and props and wingers worst (but props still
  offload well).
- **Tactics card.** Your current attack play or defence tactic shows on your player plate, with a D-pad hint.

## On the field: defence

- **Get back faster.** The defensive line starts retreating the moment a tackle is certain. Offside players sprint
  back, and stranded tacklers get up and rejoin. **Double-tap the left trigger** to retreat works again, at any point
  of the tackle.
- **Ruck penalties.** Penalties for an offside defender hit by a dummy-half pass, catching it while offside, or
  running into the dummy half before he picks the ball up. A marker standing beside the ruck instead of in front of
  it is offside.
- **Sweeper fullback.** The CPU fullback sits back (about 20 m deep early in the set, 35 m late) instead of charging
  every ruck, and joins the line near his own try line.
- **Fewer CPU scoots.** The CPU dummy half passes far more often instead of scooting at every chance.
- **Last-tackle warning.** On the opposition's last tackle, the tactics card flashes red: press Down to drop your
  wingers back.
- **Switching players.** The switch goes to the player nearest the ball carrier and skips offside players. It works
  while you're sprinting, and your new player's ring is thicker and easier to spot. The offside marker is now a thin
  ring under the player.
- **Defence tactics reset** to standard when you win the ball or the opposition starts a new set.

## Kicking

- **Kick aim line.** While charging a grubber or field goal, a line shows where the ball will go (wind and bounces
  included). Aim with the left stick.
- **Target kicks.** Hold the target-kick button to see two lines at once: **bomb** (warm colour) and **punt** (cool
  colour). Inside the opposition 30, a punt becomes a **cross-field kick** to the wing.
- **Chip kicks** go where you aim, not just straight ahead.
- **Kicker skill matters.** Good kickers get more control, better aim and higher bombs; poor kickers wobble and miss.
  Ratings and stamina shape grubbers and field goals.
- **Contested bombs.** Two chasers compete for the ball and the rest hold a line. Catching a bomb on the full can go
  wrong for a poor catcher under pressure.
- **Short kick-offs and drop-outs.** Push the aim stick up past about 70% at a kick-off or drop-out to switch to a short,
  high kick. Press **Triangle / Y** at the last moment while contesting to tap it back.
- **Position-based kicking ratings.** Halves are the best kickers, props the worst. Goal kicking is unchanged.
- **The CPU kicks properly.** It kicks on the last tackle instead of running it, goes for 40/20s, chip-and-chases and
  cross-field kicks, and bombs for a try at the end of a half.
- **Two-point field goals.** The CPU takes them from 40 m+ before half time and when the score is tight late. In golden
  point it shoots on the 4th or 5th tackle. A high shot on a field-goal kicker is penalised in front of the posts.

KICKING tab settings: Kick Aim Line, Chip Aim, Kick Skill Effect, Field Goal Aim Assist and Aim Sway, Bomb Height, Bomb
Max Hang, Cross-Field Zone, Bomb Contesters, Short Kick-Offs And Drop-Outs and how often the CPU uses cross-field kicks,
two-point field goals and short kicks.

## Rules and referees

- **Scrums are yours to run.** Pick the position (Square Left / Triangle Middle / Circle Right) within 5 seconds, then
  feed it with **Cross / A** within 10 seconds or concede a penalty. The D-pad picks a scrum play. Players outside the
  scrum who drift past the offside line give away a penalty, and you can choose to **repack** after one. When the CPU
  feeds, you pick a defensive stack.
- **Knock-on advantage.** After a knock-on, the other team must gain about 3 m or hold the ball for 5 seconds before
  advantage is over (a kick counts too). Tackled short of that: scrum at the knock-on, their feed. Knocking on
  themselves first is a double knock-on and the first one stands. No more instant six-again a second after losing the
  ball in a tackle.
- **Referee positioning.** The referee stands 10 m back on the defensive line, sprints on changeovers, follows line
  breaks and sets up properly at scrums and goal kicks. Touch judges stay on their touchlines. He also gives the
  "last tackle" signal.
- **On report.** High tackles show the referee's close-up, and some put the player **on report** with a banner under the
  scoreboard. In a Revamp career, reports lead to the judiciary.
- **HIAs.** Some head-high tackles send the tackled player off for a **Head Injury Assessment**. He's replaced on a
  free interchange, sits out 15 minutes of match time, then passes and returns or fails and is out for the match.
- **Bunker overturns.** Some held-up, knock-on, out-of-play or double-movement attempts are first given on the field,
  then the video referee overturns them and the points come off.
- **Conversion shot clock.** Time comes off after 80 seconds, and in the last 5 minutes the clock stops from the try
  until the kicker starts his run-up.
- **Field goals** get a single whistle instead of two whistles and a "Try!" call.
- **Professional fouls.** Holding down the tackler who ended a line break is a penalty and a sin bin (see
  [Discipline](discipline-system.md#professional-fouls-after-a-line-break)).
- **Ball into touch.** Instead of a referee cutscene, the player who last touched it plays a short reaction.

## The CPU (AI)

- **Uses the whole bench.** Tired forwards and the hooker come off at around half stamina, with rotations around 20
  minutes, half time, 60 and 70. Starters come back on in the second half, and the interchange limit is respected. It
  also manages your team if automatic interchanges are on.
- **Plays to the situation.** More expansive when behind late, kicks on the last, field goals when it matters.
- **Cracks under pressure.** Through the discipline meter, a rattled CPU team gives away penalties, forces offloads and
  plays the ball sloppily.

AI tab settings: AI Interchange Fatigue, AI Bench Rotation, AI Rotation Checkpoints, AI Late-Game Urgency, AI Ruck
Awareness, Fullback Depth, AI Dummy-Half Scoots and AI Own-End Slow Play-The-Ball.

## Stamina, injuries and ratings

- **Half time isn't a full refill.** Players get back part of their missing stamina, based on their fitness rating.
  Unfit forwards start the second half tired.
- **Position-based overall ratings (OVR).** A prop is rated on defence, carries and offloads, a half on kicking and
  passing, a winger on speed, catching and evasion, all on one scale. Sprint speed now counts (it didn't in stock).
- **Injury scenes.** Unused concussion and hamstring cutscenes are back, and HIAs have their own.
- **New Misc-tab sliders:** Game Speed, Pace Spread (how different fast and slow players feel), Acceleration, Fatigue &
  Pressure Errors and Half-Time Recovery.

## Career mode

Revamp careers save separately from stock ones (see the [FAQ](faq.md#will-my-old-careers-still-work)). Start a new
career to get everything below.

- **Club colours** on every career screen.
- **Theme rounds.** Special balls, referee kits and field markings for theme rounds, **Magic Round** every season,
  Super League **Magic Weekend** and an annual **Las Vegas** doubleheader.
- **Home grounds.** Your home games are spread across primary, secondary and "visit" grounds. In the career Settings
  tab, **Draw Customize** lets you set three rivals and how your home games are split (from next season).
- **Teamlist Tuesday.** A popup with your opponent's 1-17 and reserves.
- **Fans.** Fan comments in the news feed and a **Fan Pulse** page.
- **Judiciary.** Players put on report can be fined or suspended, more often for repeat offenders. Each career keeps a
  Judiciary Record.
- **Milestones.** 100th, 150th and 200th games, with the milestone player leading the team out.
- **Mid-season transfers**, with transfer news.
- **Salary cap sanctions.** Stay over the cap and you're fined weekly, and eventually the board sacks you.
- **Feeder clubs and rivals.** NRL rivals are played twice a season, Super League rivals three times.
- **Random draws and players.** On career setup: Draw (Default / Randomly Generated), Randomize Players (Off / Set
  Random / True Random), Keep Players To Same League and Randomize Loyalty.
- **The State Championship.** The QLD Cup winner plays the NSW Cup winner on Grand Final day.
- **The international calendar.** World Cups every four years. In between: the **Pacific Cup**, the **Pacific Bowl**,
  a promotion play-off between them, and England hosting a three-Test series (the **Ashes** against Australia).
- **A restyled squad screen** with player tabs and rating bars (L1 / R1 switch modes, L2 / R2 switch player tabs).
- **Be a Pro.** Movement and strength attributes can now be upgraded (OVR no longer stalls in the high 70s), upgrades
  cost half as much, and challenges match your role. Press **Y** on the Objectives screen for new challenges (once a
  week).

## Competitions and clubs

- **Perth Bears.** The NRL's 18th club, at HBF Park and North Sydney Oval, with a 2027 squad.
- **2027 squads.** Confirmed 2027 signings, departures and retirements are applied.
- **NSW Cup.** The league, its clubs and their real 2026 squads are unlocked, plus 464 reserve-grade players. It shows
  in Play Now team select and competition mode.
- **Women's clubs.** The 2026 women's clubs are unlocked.
- **One club per team.** The separate "2025" clubs are folded into the 2026 ones, so each team appears once. Their old
  kits come along as "HOME 2025" and so on.
- **Competition mode.** Custom leagues of up to **30 clubs** (stock 16), plus Pacific Cup, Pacific Bowl, International
  Test and NSW Cup tiles, and randomly generated draws.
- **Coaches.** Clubs have a head coach in a club-coloured polo, and both coaches stand on the sideline with the
  occasional reaction close-up. 70 hidden coaches and two female referees (Kasey Badger and Belinda Sharpe) are
  unlocked.
- **Corrected club colours** for 59 teams (Penrith black and red, Parramatta blue first and so on). They drive the
  scoreboard, loading screen and more.
- **Corrected stadium names** for 24 grounds.

## Play Now

- **Match Settings has four tabs** (L1 / R1): **General** (including **Finals**: play it as a Grand Final),
  **Time & Weather**, **Officiating** (choose the referee and touch judges, and how kick-off is decided: coin toss,
  random or your choice), and **Visuals** (field markings, ball, scoreboard, referee jersey, commentary, trophy and
  crowd, as picture tiles).
- **Presets.** Press **Square** to save or load your settings, or load a competition's look (NRL, NRLW, NSW Cup,
  Origin, Super League...).
- **Crowd settings.** Set attendance (0% = an empty ground) and the home / away fan split.
- **Quick team jump.** Press **Start** on the League or Team row to pick from a grid of logos.
- **Home grounds first.** Stadium Select lists both clubs' home grounds at the top. **R2 / L2** jump between letters.

## Academy and editing

- **Edit official players more.** Scanned players' body, tattoos, sleeves, strapping, headgear and boots, and every
  official player's preferred roles. Faces, names and numbers stay locked.
- **Edit official clubs.** Names, emblem, colours and home grounds.
- **Edit official kits.** Change the cut and the back numbers (font, colour, keylines), add a **name on the back**,
  and get automatic **chest text** for Grand Finals, Origin deciders and milestone games. Restore Official Jerseys
  undoes it all.
- **Kit Library.** Each club's Uniforms list has a **KIT LIBRARY** of older kits beyond the 20 slots. **COPY ACADEMY
  UNIFORM** copies any club's kit into yours, including downloaded ones.
- **Share players and teams as files.** Export a player (Square in the player view) or a team (X on the club hub),
  and import players from the Players menu. (Importing teams isn't supported yet.)
- **Referees & Coaches editor** in the Academy row: edit or create referees and coaches.
- **Kicking tee colour.** Player editor > Appearance > Accessories > Kicking Tee.
- **12 patterned headgear designs** (lightning, tiger, tribal, camo, flames and more).
- **Custom difficulties** can be exported and imported as files (Square).
- **Clear Academy Data.** Press Y / Triangle on the Academy row to bulk-delete or reset Academy content. It asks first
  and can't be undone.
- **More emblems.** North Sydney Bears, PNG Chiefs, classic NRL logos and 55 hidden badges are unlocked in the emblem
  picker.
- **Trophy Viewer** in Extras: every trophy on its stand.

## Broadcast presentation

- **NRL-style scoreboard** with crests, the half, the clock and a tackle counter. Animated try, sin bin, send-off, on
  report and interchange banners, and a TV-style stat banner during play. The player plate, interchange screen,
  penalty choice and team lists are restyled to match. Super League gets a Sky-style board and the NSW Cup gets its own.
- **Versus intro.** Matches open like a TV broadcast: stadium shots, crests flying in, the venue and kick-off time, then
  a team-colour swipe into the sheds.
- **Coin toss.** The away captain calls, a 3D coin flips (it matches the competition), and the winner chooses to kick
  off or pick ends. Use **Left / Right** and **Cross / A**.
- **Grand Final intros.** Club-colour tunnel lights, crowd light shows in team colours, post-fireworks smoke, and a
  choice of RL26's intro, a trailer-style intro or **Rugby League Live 4's tunnel walk**.
- **Anzac Round.** Both teams walk out together, then line up for the Last Post.
- **Bunker decisions.** A logo wipe into the replay, "Decision pending", then a big **TRY** (green) or **NO TRY**
  (red). A try now gets the whistle, the call and a celebration.
- **Replay stinger.** A team-colour swipe with the club crest leads from a try into its replay.
- **Match summary.** Penalty goals are labelled properly, scorers are listed in order, and the team sheets show
  numbers and colours.
- **Finals screens.** The stadium's big screen turns gold for Grand Finals and silver for other finals.
- **Ladder logos.** Club logos on the competition ladder (empty in stock).
- **Loading screen.** Club-colour arrows, bigger crests, the stadium in the background and a spinning ball.
- **Try celebrations.** Celebrations that shipped but never played are back (including Melbourne Storm players' own,
  with teammates joining in), plus new ones for Hamiso Tabuai-Fidow and Kotoni Staggs. About 225 unused gameplay
  animations are mixed in too.
- **Players smile in the menus** instead of staring blankly.

## Music and sound

- **Team songs.** Every NRL club has a run-out song and a winning song through the stadium PA. Both teams' songs play
  at Grand Finals, finals, Magic Round and Las Vegas. Turn songs on or off per club in **Settings > Audio > Manage
  Music** (press Square on a row to preview).
- **Try songs** for the Broncos and Roosters.
- **NRL siren** at half time and full time (UK grounds keep their own).
- **Menu music playlists.** Choose **Non-Copyrighted (Stream Safe)** (the default) or **Copyrighted** footy anthems in
  **Settings > Audio > Manage Music**, and switch tracks on or off.

## Stadiums and the pitch

- **Realistic grass.** Broadcast-green turf, winter ryegrass at cold grounds, couch grass in the tropics and tired
  turf at suburban parks.
- **Field markings.** NRL-style sponsor markings and round branding on the grass, set automatically in career.
  Rounds include ANZAC, Indigenous, Women in League, Multicultural, Magic Round, Country Round, Finals, Grand Final,
  Origin and Las Vegas. Pick one in Play Now's Field Markings.
- **Pitch numbers** in a bold broadcast-style font.
- **LED boards.** Each club's own partners on the stadium ribbons, with special boards for Grand Finals, finals, Origin,
  Magic Round and Las Vegas.
- **Goal post pads** for more clubs, and red Harvey Norman World Cup posts.
- **Las Vegas (Allegiant Stadium).** Mustard NFL-style posts, black in-goals and Rugby League Las Vegas branding.
- **Suncorp Stadium.** The roof lights take on the teams' colours at night games.
- **Sunshine Coast Stadium** is selectable, in an early version of the real ground.

## Crowds

- **Fans wear their team's jerseys and merch:** tees, polos, hoodies, scarves, caps and beanies, current and classic.
  At Grand Finals the glowing wristbands light up in team colours.
- **Fans wave team flags.**
- **Crowd Variance** (Settings > Graphics) sets how much variety there is. High uses more video memory.
- **Fangear Library** (Academy club > Uniforms) lets you switch individual fan designs on or off.

- **Hundreds of extra kits.** Every hidden kit that ships with the game is unlocked, plus over 300 older kits (2017 to
  2024, from Rugby League Live 4 and its kit packs) in the Kit Library.
- **Kit names show their season** ("HOME 2026"), and clubs wear their newest kit by default.
- **Fixed logos** on a few official kits.
- **Seven new referee kits** for 2026 (Play Now > Officiating > Referee Jersey).
- **Nine special balls:** Magic Round, NSW Cup, NRLW and NRL Grand Final, Ashes, Pacific Test, Super League Las
  Vegas and Magic Weekend, and an RL26 Revamped ball. Career uses them automatically.
- **96 HD club logos.**
- **A cleaner World Cup 2026 logo.**

## Menus and look

- **New home screen** with four tabs (Play, Academy, Settings, Extras; LB / RB to switch) and a random NRL player in
  his club's kit.
- **Revamp theme.** Flat dark panels and Revamp blue across the menus (your club's colours in career).
- **New pause menu** with the score and scorers.
- **Animated boot intro and start screen.** To boot straight to the main menu, set `skip_intro = true` in
  `rl26_revamp.ini`, or tick "Skip the boot splash screens" in the launcher.
- **PlayStation button prompts** if a PlayStation pad is plugged in before you press Play (or choose in the launcher).
- **About RL26 Revamped** in Extras.

## Graphics

- **Settings > Graphics** is a new page with every graphics option, including quality rows the PC version never
  showed: Graphics Preset, Display, Quality (shadows, anti-aliasing, bloom, ambient occlusion, grass, crowd and more)
  and FPS Cap (Off, 30, 60, 100, 144, 240).
- **Revamp Lighting.** A more filmic look with a new tonemap curve and colour grade. Choose from 16 colour grades and
  adjust exposure, saturation, vignette, ambient occlusion, bloom, sunlight and more. **Restore Lighting Defaults**
  puts it back. Changes apply from the next match.
- **More memory for the game**, fixing a crash when quitting a match.

## Online

**Online play is switched off.** Gameplay mods would make your game behave differently from another player's, and
playing online with a modded game risks your account. The Online box (and Pro Team) is removed from the menu, and
invites are cancelled with a message. You can still browse and download Academy content. Before you **upload**
anything, the Revamp warns you that it may include kits, logos or players other people don't have.

## The optional Rugby 25 Expansion

If you own **Rugby 25** on Steam, the launcher can add the Rugby 25 Expansion. It's converted on your PC from your own
copy. It adds:

- about 100 extra tackle animations, plus new high shots, big fends, sidesteps, bumps and in-goal tackles;
- four new try celebrations and post-conversion reactions;
- the referee having a word with the offender before some penalties;
- two extra tunnel walks;
- extra facial expressions.

Add or remove it at any time from the launcher.
